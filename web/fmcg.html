Shift planning & production execution
Plans shift targets, line allocation, changeovers and execution against the daily production plan.		3Understands assigned tasks but needs close guidance to meet the shift plan.Follows the shift plan and records output against hourly targets.Reallocates people and sequences work to recover plan losses and manage changeovers.Coordinates multiple lines/shifts and balances capacity against changing demand.
OEE & productivity management
Tracks availability, performance and quality losses; uses downtime and output data to improve line efficiency.		3Recognises output and downtime figures but cannot explain the main losses.Records hourly output, downtime and rejects; calculates basic OEE components with support.Analyses Pareto losses, leads action tracking and verifies productivity improvements.Drives sustained OEE improvement across lines and aligns maintenance/production priorities.
Quality & food safety
Maintains GMP, hygiene, process controls, traceability and product-quality standards.		4Knows basic hygiene rules but needs reminders to follow all checks.Completes routine quality and hygiene checks and escalates deviations.Prevents recurrence through root-cause actions and ensures documentation is complete.Leads audit readiness and quality culture; manages critical deviations and cross-functional closure.
EHS & safe work practices
Ensures PPE, machine guarding, LOTO, safe material handling and incident/near-miss reporting.		4Follows safety instructions when supervised.Uses required PPE and follows standard safe-work procedures.Identifies hazards, stops unsafe work and closes corrective actions with the team.Leads risk reviews and safety improvement plans across shifts/areas.
Manpower leadership & communication
Allocates operators, conducts shift briefings, coaches employees and manages attendance/discipline.		3Works with the team but needs support to allocate tasks or communicate priorities.Allocates daily tasks and communicates targets during shift handover.Coaches operators, handles routine conflicts and maintains skill/attendance coverage.Builds a flexible team and develops future line leaders across shifts.
Problem solving & continuous improvement
Uses 5 Why, fishbone, basic RCA, 5S and Kaizen to eliminate recurring shop-floor losses.		3Reports problems but relies on others to identify causes and actions.Participates in 5S and problem-solving activities using a structured format.Leads RCA, assigns countermeasures and checks effectiveness at the workplace.Sponsors cross-line improvement projects and sustains measurable gains.
Material & inventory control
Controls RM/PM availability, line feeding, WIP, FIFO/FEFO and material reconciliation.		2Identifies materials but needs help with stock checks and issue records.Checks material availability, follows FIFO/FEFO and records consumption accurately.Prevents shortages and excess WIP through coordination with stores and planning.Optimises material flow and inventory controls across production areas.
Production reporting & digital systems
Maintains shift reports, downtime logs, production registers and basic ERP/MES/Excel entries.		2Completes basic manual records with frequent corrections.Updates shift reports and enters production data accurately and on time.Validates data, prepares trend reports and uses dashboards to trigger actions.Standardises digital reporting and coaches teams on data accuracy and system use.
